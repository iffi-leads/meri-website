FAQs

Helpful things to know.

Which study modes are available?

The registration form lists virtual learning and classroom options. Confirm the mode available for your selected program with admissions.

What documents will I need?

Applicants may need proof of identity, a recent photo, education documents and payment evidence. See the registration form for details.

Does submitting an application confirm admission?

No. Applications are reviewed by IOCH. Admission is confirmed only after the relevant review and approval process.

How can I verify a certificate?

Enter the certificate ID in the verification form above. Verification requires the live IOCH certificate register.
